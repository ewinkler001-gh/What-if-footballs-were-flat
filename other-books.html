Children's Books Beyond Football

While Flatfootballs began with a love of football and sports-themed children's books, Eric R. Winkler also creates stories that explore imagination, friendship, courage, kindness, and the experiences that help children grow.

Explore the children's books below and discover stories designed to entertain young readers while sharing positive and meaningful messages.

Missy the Unicorn

Written and illustrated by Eric R. Winkler
Concept by Melissa J. Medina

Missy the Unicorn is a touching picture book about a happy little unicorn whose magical gift suddenly disappears.

With the help of Papa Gnome, Missy learns that even the most magical unicorns sometimes need a little help—and that's perfectly okay.

The story encourages children to believe in themselves, accept help, build resilience, and discover the courage to shine again.

Perfect for ages 3–8
24-page children's picture book
ISBN: 9798190463897
